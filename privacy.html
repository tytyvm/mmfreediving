Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

This policy explains how MM Freediving handles your information when you contact us by phone or text message about our freediving courses and bookings. MM Freediving is a freediving instruction business operated as a sole proprietorship in Jupiter / Palm Beach Gardens, Florida.

We do not share, sell, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent information to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Information we collect

  • Your mobile phone number, so we can reply to you.
  • The content of the calls, voicemails, and text messages you send us.
  • Details you choose to share, such as your name, course interest, preferred dates, or party size.

We do not buy phone numbers or use purchased marketing lists. You hear from us only because you contacted MM Freediving first.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your inquiry and answer questions about our courses, schedule, and pricing.
  • To help you book and to send you a booking link or confirmation.
  • To follow up about a booking or course you asked about.

We send text messages only in response to your contact with us. We use a messaging service provider solely to deliver these texts on our behalf; it may not use your information for any other purpose.

Message frequency & rates

Message frequency varies and depends on your conversation with us. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

Opting out

You can stop messages at any time by replying STOP to any text. You will receive one confirmation and then no further messages unless you contact us again. Reply HELP for help.

Data retention & security

We keep your conversation only as long as needed to provide the service and meet any legal obligations, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it.
