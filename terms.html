Program name

MM Freediving text messaging. MM Freediving is a freediving instruction business operated as a sole proprietorship in Jupiter / Palm Beach Gardens, Florida.

Program description

When you call or text MM Freediving, you may receive text-message replies from us about our freediving courses, scheduling, pricing, and bookings. You receive these messages because you contacted MM Freediving first; your consent is given directly to MM Freediving and is not shared with anyone.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies and depends on your conversation. You will generally receive messages only in response to your own.

Cost

MM Freediving does not charge you to receive these messages. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.

HELP: Reply HELP to any message for assistance, or email mmfreediving@gmail.com.

STOP: Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive a one-time confirmation and then no further messages unless you contact us again.
